# KOTIKI X-HELP — Privacy and Policy Change Notices

Status: proposal / implementation specification. This document does not replace a reviewed privacy policy and does not mean that a notification feature is already implemented.

## Goal

Give users a clear, dated record of important changes to privacy practices, terms, security behavior, and product functionality.

## What a notice should include

- **Change title**
- **Published date and effective date**
- **Affected product(s)**
- **What changed** — a short, plain-language summary
- **Why it changed** — the reason, if known and appropriate to disclose
- **Data impact** — what data is newly collected, no longer collected, retained differently, or shared differently; say “no known data-handling change” only after verification
- **User action** — clearly state whether users need to do anything
- **Full policy link** — the actual reviewed policy, not a guessed URL
- **Contact/report route** — a working, monitored contact channel

## Publication principles

1. Do not announce a policy change before the updated policy has been reviewed and is ready to publish.
2. Show the effective date and retain previous versions or a change history.
3. Separate editorial corrections from material changes that affect users' rights, choices, or data handling.
4. Explain material changes directly; do not hide them only inside a long legal document.
5. Do not say that a change is legally compliant in every country without appropriate review.
6. Collect only the information needed to send notices. Avoid exposing recipients or using private user data in public update posts.
7. Never fabricate a change, an incident, an approval, or a user impact.

## Suggested public changelog entry

> **[Product] — Privacy notice update**
>
> Published: [date]
>
> Effective: [date]
>
> **Summary:** [plain-language description of the verified change].
>
> **Why:** [reason].
>
> **Data impact:** [specific verified impact, or state that the assessment is still in progress].
>
> **Do you need to do anything?** [yes/no, with a clear explanation].
>
> Read the updated policy: [verified link].

## Notification delivery

When implemented, use an in-product banner or notice feed for important updates, and an additional trusted channel for urgent security incidents. Do not assume that an email or in-app message was delivered unless delivery is confirmed. Allow users to distinguish urgent security alerts from ordinary release notes.

## Review checklist

- [ ] The facts and dates have been checked.
- [ ] The policy text and link are final and accessible.
- [ ] The data impact and required user action are explained plainly.
- [ ] The notice does not reveal sensitive technical or personal information.
- [ ] A responsible reviewer has approved the notice.
- [ ] The previous version and publication timestamp are retained.
- [ ] The delivery mechanism has been tested.
