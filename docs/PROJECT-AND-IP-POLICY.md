# Project Identity and Intellectual Property Handling

**Working policy — not legal advice.** Review with a qualified adult/legal professional before using this as a public legal policy or agreement.

## 1. Project identity

KOTIKI X LABS is currently a developing voluntary technology initiative name. Unless and until a legal entity is actually established, public materials must not imply that it is an incorporated company, registered charity, or other formal legal person.

A project name or team label does not, by itself, create a separate legal entity or automatically shield its operator or contributors from responsibility.

## 2. Official projects and independent participants

Only projects listed by the maintainer in an official project index or repository should be presented as official KOTIKI X LABS releases. Independent users and community members do not automatically become team representatives, employees, agents, partners, or authorized spokespeople.

No one may use the brand to imply approval or authority they have not received. This does not remove any responsibilities that applicable law imposes.

## 3. Source and rights register

Before publishing a tool, maintain a record of:
- Project name, repository, version, and release date
- Authors and contributors
- Source code origin and applicable license
- Third-party packages, fonts, icons, images, datasets, and other assets
- Required notices, attribution, and license obligations
- Any written agreement about ownership or permission to use contributions
- Maintainer responsible for reviewing the record

Do not publish material with unknown provenance until rights are clarified.

## 4. Contributions and ownership

Copyright ownership and permissions depend on applicable law, the facts, and any valid agreement. A contribution does not automatically transfer ownership merely because it is submitted to a repository.

For collaborative projects, agree in writing before combining substantial work on:
- Who owns which contributions
- What license permits the project to distribute and modify the work
- Attribution requirements
- Whether the work may be reused in other projects
- Any compensation or other commitments, if applicable

Do not claim exclusive ownership of third-party or openly licensed material beyond the rights actually granted.

## 5. Trademarks and branding

Use the KOTIKI X LABS name and logo only in ways authorized by the project maintainer. Permission to contribute code is not permission to register, sell, or impersonate the brand. Do not describe the name as a registered trademark unless registration has been confirmed in the relevant jurisdiction.

## 6. Takedown and disputes

A person who believes a release unlawfully uses their work should provide the exact URL/file, identify the material, explain the concern, and provide a safe contact method. The maintainer will review the request and may temporarily remove or disable disputed material while checking it.

Do not publish personal information or make public accusations before facts are reviewed. This policy does not decide legal disputes or replace applicable notice-and-takedown procedures.

## 7. No blanket waiver

Nothing here guarantees immunity from claims or excludes liability that cannot legally be excluded. Mandatory rights under applicable law remain in place.
